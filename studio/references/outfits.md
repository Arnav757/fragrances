# Outfit reference library

Mood board the founder supplied. Use it whenever we write ChatGPT image prompts, Gemini animation
prompts or reel scripts. **Take the clothes, fits, colours and styling from these refs, never the
people:** several photos show real, recognisable men. Prompts describe garments on our own characters
(Arjun, Kabir, …) only.

Refs are numbered in the order they were sent (R1–R25). R1–R5 and R16–R25 are saved locally as
`studio/work/references/r<N>.*` (git-ignored, since they're other people's photos).
Leave visible logos (team caps, polo-player emblems) out of prompts: use plain, unbranded versions.

## The house look (true of almost every ref)

1. **Long leg line.** Trousers are full length and straight, wide, bootcut or flared. The hem breaks
   on or pools over the shoe. Never skinny, tapered or cropped.
2. **Short or tucked top.** Tops are tucked, half-tucked, or cut boxy and stop at the belt, so the
   legs read long. Rise is natural to high.
3. **Leather on the feet.** Loafers (plain or woven), derbies, Chelsea or Western boots in black,
   cognac, burgundy or tan suede. Exceptions: a slim, clean white leather sneaker (R19), a low-profile
   black suede terrace sneaker (R23), and black flip-flops for the summer lane (R14, R21).
   No chunky sneakers.
4. **Quiet palette.** Indigo, light blue, navy, charcoal, grey, black, white, ecru. Colour comes from
   brown leather accents, never from loud prints. Patterns stay small: gingham, pinstripe.
5. **One texture piece.** Linen shirt, slub cotton tee or a fuzzy knit carries the outfit.
6. **Layer peek.** A white tee hem shows under a crewneck; a shirt collar and cuffs show under a knit.
7. **Undone details.** Sleeves rolled or pushed up, top two or three buttons open, sunglasses hung in
   the placket.
8. **Accessories.** Dark or tortoiseshell sunglasses, thin gold or silver chain, silver rings, steel
   or leather-strap watch, an oversized leather or canvas tote.

## Prompt building blocks

Copy these into ChatGPT image prompts, after the character description:

- Fit line: `full-length wide-leg trousers with the hem breaking over the shoes, natural-to-high
  rise, top tucked in (or boxy and ending at the belt), long unbroken leg line`
- Riviera: `{colour} linen shirt, top three buttons open, sleeves rolled to mid-forearm, tucked into
  {trouser}, {shoe}, thin gold chain, tortoiseshell sunglasses hooked into the placket`
- Knit: `{colour} soft brushed-wool crewneck with a crisp white t-shirt hem showing 2 cm below it,
  {trouser}, {shoe}, silver ring, black leather-strap watch`
- Tonal: `head-to-toe {colour}: fine-knit crewneck, pleated wide trousers pooling over {shoe},
  oversized {colour} leather tote on the shoulder`
- Photo style: `candid street-style photo, natural daylight, shot at hip height on a 35mm lens,
  textured stone or plaster wall behind, slight film grain, not posed like a catalogue`

Gemini animation cues that suit these looks: a slow walk past the camera so the wide trousers swing;
rolling up a sleeve; tucking sunglasses into the placket; tugging the knit hem down over the tee.
